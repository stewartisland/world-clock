World Clock {VERSION} for Windows
==============================

The time and weather in the places you care about, at a glance.

INSTALL
  1. Extract this zip to a folder (right-click > Extract All...).
  2. Double-click Install.cmd.
  3. World Clock opens, and it's added to your Start menu.

  No administrator rights are needed, and .NET doesn't need to be installed:
  everything is inside WorldClock.exe. It installs to
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\World Clock.

  "Windows protected your PC"? The app isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn
  you the first time. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

  You can also skip installing: WorldClock.exe runs on its own from any folder.

BRING YOUR CLOCKS FROM ANOTHER PC
  Clocks are saved in %APPDATA%\WorldClock\clocks.json. To copy your setup,
  close World Clock on both PCs and copy that file across.

UNINSTALL
  Settings > Apps > Installed apps > World Clock > Uninstall,
  or run Uninstall.cmd. Your saved clocks are kept unless you choose to delete them.

REQUIREMENTS
  Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Internet access for weather and city search.
